Who We Are

One umbrella organization, built to last beyond a single visit

BrightBridge Global Initiative is a student-led educational initiative founded by three students from Troy High School — Zichong Xu, Brian Li, and Joreme Liu. Beginning in Laos, BrightBridge works with underserved schools to expand access to English, computer science, STEM, and arts education through student-led instruction, refurbished technology, offline learning resources, and partnerships with local educators.

Rather than focusing on short-term donations, BrightBridge aims to build sustainable learning programs that remain in local communities after each project ends. Laos is the first pilot — the model is designed to be replicated, evaluated, and sustained in future communities.

1

Research

Identify 1–2 schools and assess student enrollment and age, English proficiency, devices and connectivity, electricity, teaching staff, classrooms, and equipment storage capacity.

2

Build

Develop English, CS/STEM, and art curricula; build the Offline Learning System and teaching materials; and collect, test, and refurbish equipment.

3

Laos Pilot

On site, the team follows a clear sequence — Teach → Install → Train → Create → Assess — rather than reducing the visit to a few isolated lessons.

4

Continue Online

After returning to the United States, the connection continues: Learning Buddies, online classes, teacher support, and student projects.

5

Measure

Track students served, teaching hours, equipment, teacher training, student projects, pre/post assessment results, and continued use of the equipment and curriculum.

Success is not defined by having visited Laos. It is defined by whether the school can continue offering classes after the team leaves.

Programs

Three programs, one connected model

Digital learning, on-site resources and technology, and a long-term academic bridge reinforce one another — content, equipment, relationships, and continuing support.

Technology Flagship

Bridge to Code — Offline Learning System

A low-cost offline learning system for schools with limited internet access. It runs through a USB drive, a single laptop, or a local network, so students can keep accessing lessons, exercises, quizzes, and projects even when internet service is unreliable.

  • Lesson Videos & PDF Lessons
  • English Lessons
  • Coding Exercises
  • Art Tutorials
  • Quizzes & Student Projects
  • Learning Progress
Global Learning Community

Learning Buddies

Learning Buddies turns one visit into ongoing shared learning. Troy High students and Laos students build two-way connections through language, technology, art, and collaborative projects — and present their work together at the annual BrightBridge Global Showcase.

  • English Conversation
  • Cultural Exchange
  • STEM & Coding Projects
  • Art Exchange
  • Online Workshops
  • Annual Showcase
In the Classroom

English for STEM & Art & Creativity

Two curriculum tracks run side by side with Bridge to Code. In English for STEM, students learn English through technology — and technology through English: computer basics, typing, presentations, coding, STEM vocabulary, and digital research. The Art & Creativity program brings drawing, design, and digital art into the same classrooms, with artwork exchanges and peer feedback between Troy and Laos students.

  • Computer Basics & Typing
  • STEM Vocabulary
  • Digital Research
  • Drawing & Design
  • Digital Art
  • Student Presentations
